Mathematical Invariant:

Bilateral Monotonicity: The array must be monotonic (sorted or strictly non-decreasing). When moving pointer L to the right strictly increases sum, and moving pointer R to the left strictly decreases sum, candidate pairs outside the bounding window can be pruned in O(1) without evaluation.
