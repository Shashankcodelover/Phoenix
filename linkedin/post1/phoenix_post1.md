# 🎯 Introducing Phoenix: The Full-Stack AI Career Accelerator & Hackathon Engine

Navigating high-stakes technical interviews and fast-paced hackathons requires more than static practice questions — it demands active, personalized feedback and battle-tested execution strategies. I built **Phoenix** to bridge this gap with an autonomous, real-time coaching ecosystem.

### 🛠️ What I Built & Deployed:
• **Autonomous Mock Interview Simulator**: Interactive technical and behavioral interview sessions featuring instant response scoring, STAR method breakdown, and technical depth evaluations.
• **Hackathon Strategy Blueprint Engine**: Automated generation of complete hackathon project roadmaps, judging criterion alignment, tech stack selection, and rapid prototyping workflows.
• **Readiness Telemetry & Analytics**: Real-time candidate confidence gauge and multi-axial weakness radar that pinpoints conceptual gaps and generates tailored revision paths.
• **High-Performance Architecture**: Fast, modern interface built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS, engineered for low-latency feedback and frictionless developer navigation.
