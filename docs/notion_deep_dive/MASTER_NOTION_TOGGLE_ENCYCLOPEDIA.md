<summary><b>2.2 Core Technical Moats & Mathematical Formulations</b></summary>

#### Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN) Epidemic Bundle Routing
When disaster nodes operate without continuous internet connectivity, messages are routed via Store-Carry-and-Forward epidemic bundles. Message vectors are reconciled using Bloom Filters and Merkle trees:
$$P(\text{Delivery}) = 1 - \prod_{k=1}^{R} (1 - p_k)^{\Delta t}$$
where $p_k$ is the peer-to-peer encounter probability and $R$ is the number of mobile carrier nodes.

#### Kalman-Filtered GPS Geofence Verification
Raw emergency beacon coordinates $(\tilde{x}_t, \tilde{y}_t)$ contaminated by severe weather interference are filtered before triggering resource drops:
$$\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t|t} = \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t|t-1} + \mathbf{K}_t (\mathbf{z}_t - \mathbf{H} \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t|t-1})$$
$$\mathbf{K}_t = \mathbf{P}_{t|t-1} \mathbf{H}^T (\mathbf{H} \mathbf{P}_{t|t-1} \mathbf{H}^T + \mathbf{R})^{-1}$$

#### Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon Geofencing
To verify if an emergency beacon lies within an active hazard zone:
$$\text{Inside}(P) = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{I}\left( (y_i > y) \neq (y_{i+1} > y) \land x < \frac{x_{i+1}-x_i}{y_{i+1}-y_i}(y - y_i) + x_i \right) \right) \pmod 2 \equiv 1$$

<summary><b>3.2 Mathematical Optimization Formulation</b></summary>

#### Integer Linear Programming (ILP) Formulation
Let $x_{i,j,t} \in \{0, 1\}$ be the binary decision variable indicating student $i$ is assigned to company $j$ at timeslot $t$.
$$\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{S} \sum_{j=1}^{C} \sum_{t=1}^{T} w_{i,j} \cdot x_{i,j,t} - \lambda \sum \text{Penalty}$$

Subject to:
1. **No Student Overlap**: $\sum_{j=1}^{C} x_{i,j,t} \le 1 \quad \forall i, t$
2. **Company Interviewer Concurrency**: $\sum_{i=1}^{S} x_{i,j,t} \le K_j \quad \forall j, t$
3. **Daily Interview Quota**: $\sum_{t=1}^{T} x_{i,j,t} \le 1 \quad \forall i, j$

#### Kuhn-Munkres (Hungarian) Maximum Weight Bipartite Matching
Time complexity: $O(V^3)$. Computes optimal bottleneck-free pairings by transforming the adjacency cost matrix into a feasible vertex labeling:
$$l(u) + l(v) \ge w(u, v) \quad \forall u \in U, v \in V$$

</details>

<summary><b>4.2 Security Mathematics & Anti-Spoofing Proofs</b></summary>

#### Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Liveness Formula
To detect photo cutouts and video playback spoofing:
$$\text{EAR} = \frac{\|p_2 - p_6\| + \|p_3 - p_5\|}{2 \|p_1 - p_4\|}$$
A blink is registered when $\text{EAR} < \theta_{\text{blink}}$ for duration $\Delta t \in [100\text{ms}, 400\text{ms}]$, verified alongside active head yaw/pitch vector changes.

#### Dynamic TOTP Bleed Mitigation
To prevent shoulder-surfing and QR replay attacks, tokens expire dynamically on scan using HMAC-SHA256:
$$\text{Token} = \text{Truncate}(\text{HMAC-SHA256}(K, \lfloor T / T_0 \rfloor)) \oplus \text{Non-Reversible Nonce}$$
